Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

TaliHub is a personal app used only by its owner. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, TaliHub accesses the owner's Google Calendar: calendar names, events, times, titles, locations and descriptions, and it may create or change events when the owner asks it to.

How the data is used

The calendar data is used only to show the owner's schedule and give reminders inside TaliHub. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models, and it is not sold.

Storage and sharing

Data and sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party, and no data is shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting TaliHub's local files removes all stored data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TaliHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
